## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: #f2e39e to evoke feelings of sophistication and elegance, drawing from the logo's golden hue.
- Background color: Black, aligning with the logo's background to create a sleek, modern look.
- Primary Dark: #b9ae75 for interactive elements and accents, providing a subtle contrast against the primary color.
- Headline font: 'Belleza' (sans-serif) for headlines and shorter text snippets, offering a touch of elegance. Body font: 'Alegreya' (serif) for body text, ensuring readability with a classic appeal.
- Use elegant and minimalist icons for services and navigation, in a style reminiscent of high-end fashion and beauty brands. Favor line icons with rounded terminals over filled icons.
- A clean, grid-based layout with ample white space to showcase portfolio images effectively. Prioritize visual content with large, high-quality images and videos against the black background.
- Subtle transition effects and animations to enhance user engagement. For example, a fade-in effect when loading new images in the portfolio or a smooth scroll to different sections of the website.
